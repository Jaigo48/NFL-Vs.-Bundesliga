# Spain and Italy exhibit strong duopolies, while England has a shared dominance at the top but a more distributed history overall.

## Spain (La Liga): Duopoly

Real Madrid and Barcelona form a definitive duopoly.

Together, they have won 65 of the 95 total championships awarded since the league's inception in 1929.

This represents approximately **68%** of all titles.

## Italy (Serie A): Dominant Duo

Juventus and Inter Milan hold a commanding lead.  

Combined, they possess 57 titles out of roughly 123 contested seasons **(approx.  46%)**.
